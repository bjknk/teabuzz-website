03 / THE STORY BEHIND THE BUZZ

One idea.
A vision beyond
borders.

I'm Kudakwashe Kayde Dhani, a young Zimbabwean founder and the person behind TeaBuzz.

I started with a question: what if students had one place to share their experiences, discover opportunities, find their people and connect beyond the boundaries of their own campus?

That question became TeaBuzz — a student-focused social platform built around the moments, interests and connections that make student life what it is. What began as one idea is now a bigger ambition: to connect students across universities, communities and countries.

Building TeaBuzz from Zimbabwe reflects something I believe deeply: meaningful ideas and technology can come from anywhere, and their impact doesn't have to stop at borders.

Built from Zimbabwe. Created for students everywhere. ✦
— Kudakwashe Kayde Dhani, Founder of TeaBuzz
